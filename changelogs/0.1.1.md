# LiquidAss 0.1.1

- Adds the isolated iOS 12 SpringBoard Metal glass test surface.
- Uses a real wallpaper/window snapshot as the backdrop source for refraction,
  blur, bezel, tint, and specular shader work.
- Quarantines existing stock-UI hook families on iOS 12 until the standalone
  renderer is confirmed working on-device.
- Adds explicit QuartzCore registration and Metal pipeline diagnostics.
- Keeps the iOS 13–16 renderer behavior unchanged.
- Builds rootful for iOS 12 arm64 and automatically ad-hoc signs the tweak,
  helper dylibs, and preference bundle executable.
